24/7 HallowPeen TV

Always-on cartoon channel. The crew moved into a haunted cul-de-sac for Halloween. They carve pumpkins, hand out candy, rake leaves and light graveyard candles. One of them is NOT here for the candy. 👀

  • 🎃 Every real $HPEEN buy moves a new crewmate into the neighborhood (max 25, after that buys boost the tasks bar).
  • 😈 Real sells make the impostor faster and more likely to sabotage the lights.
  • 🍬 Big buys trigger candy rain or the Pumpkin King parade.
  • 🚨 Bodies get reported, the crew argues, somebody gets yeeted. Tasks bar full = crew wins. Too many ghosts = impostor wins. Then it all restarts.

This is an animated show, not a person live-streaming. Trades are read from DexScreener's public data roughly every 45 seconds, so the action can lag a bit behind the chart. When the feed is quiet, the neighborhood just keeps doing silly Halloween tasks — no fake buys are invented. Crewmates get silly random names, never wallet addresses. Nothing here is financial advice; $HPEEN is a meme coin for entertainment only. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Innersloth or Among Us; all art and sounds are original.

📝 Crewmate names

Names typed into the "Name a crewmate" box get checked for bad words and slurs, length, and wallet-looking strings. On your own screen the name moves in right away. There is no server, so names never leave the browser that typed them. For names that should show up for everyone, add them to the NAMES list at the top of live/sim.js.

🎆 Halloween night finale

At midnight on Oct 31 (viewer's local time), the current round ends and a giant finale round starts with a full 25-crewmate neighborhood, 2 impostors, and fireworks, followed by a "Season 1 complete" screen with stats. Preview it with ?finale=1.
